About Jalsetu (WDMS)

Intelligent Water Demand Management System for Municipal Operations

1. Purpose & Core Objective

Jalsetu bridges historical water consumption patterns and forward-looking operational decisions. Built specifically for water operations officers and municipal planners, it transforms raw sensor telemetry and meter datasets into actionable redistributions.

2. Core Methodology & Algorithms

  • Demand Forecasting: LightGBM global regressor using lag profiles (1h, 24h, 168h), cyclical time features, and weather covariates to beat seasonal-naive baselines.
  • Anomaly Scoring: Residual-based robust z-score scoring using Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) with dedicated surge thresholding for rapid pipe bursts or unauthorized draws.
  • Shortage Estimation: Formulated as shortage = max(0, predicted_demand - available_supply), categorizing zones into Low, Medium, and High risk.
  • Supply Redistribution: Weighted progressive water-filling that respects capacity constraints, caps each zone at its expected demand, and prioritizes critical risk regions.

3. Data Integrity & Verification

This live frontend is built against the canonical dataset model specified in PRD.md and verified against the official UI reference mockup. Demonstration data is strictly labeled and adheres to zero-leakage, time-split verification standards.
